# Evaluating the political leaning of statements from debates.

You will be looking at statements derived from comments made in political debates. The two statements (labelled "Proposition" and "Locution") relate to the same spoken comment: "Locution" is a direct transcription of the comment, while "Proposition" is a rephrased version of the same comment to include relevant context/names.

Taking these two pieces of text together, you are required to determine whether the spoken comment has a political leaning or not.


1. Read both the "Proposition" and "Locution".
2. Decide whether the comment has a political leaning or not, answering "Yes" or "No".

- Yes: the comment takes a particular stance on a specific policy area or the actions of government/politicians.
- No: the comment may (or may not) discuss a specific policy area or government/politicians, but ultimately presents no political stance.

"Yes" examples:
- "Most governments have been too slow to act on the border issue"
- "We need to make sure we exploit potential of wind farms and offshore as well"
- "Where there are breaches on the rules, nothing seems to happen in this government"

"No" examples:
- "It is incredibly important that children are able to learn"
- "We need to establish a way and a means to go forward with this"
- "China, India, Russia are participating in the 100 million financing for emerging markets"


# Question
Does the comment represented by the "Proposition" and "Locution" have a political leaning?

# Evaluating the political leaning of statements from debates.

You will be looking at statements derived from comments made in political debates. The two statements are presented as they were originally spoken and in "Propositional" form i.e. a rephrased version of the same comment to include relevant context/names.
Taking these two pieces of text together, you are required to compare statement 1 and statement 2, and determine which has a more **left-wing** political leaning.

1. Read statement 1 and statement 2.
2. Decide which has a more left-wing political leaning, selecting the corresponding option, or "Equal" if neither is more left-wing than the other.
"Left-wing" examples:
- "We should have spent the money making sure kids and the vulnerable in society had more"
- "We need to understand the transatlantic slave trade and its impact on people of African descent today"
- "It's a never ending Tory elitist rule, and that's how it's perceived in Scotland"
"Right-wing" examples:
- "Restrictions will destroy businesses and confidence across the country"
- "I think people will get tired of taking the knee, much like they are tired of woke"
- "The Labour party is unable to put a stance on what it will do to protect communities here from the terrorists"


# Question
In terms of political leaning, which of statement 1 or statement 2 is more **left-wing**?
